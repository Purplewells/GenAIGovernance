# AI Governance RACI

## 1. Purpose

This RACI defines who is **Responsible**, **Accountable**, **Consulted**, and **Informed** for core AI governance activities.

**Legend:**

- **R** - Responsible for doing the work
- **A** - Accountable for the outcome or decision; one primary accountable role per activity
- **C** - Consulted before the activity or decision
- **I** - Informed of the result

The RACI supports, but does not replace, delegated authority, professional accountability, committee terms of reference, contracts, or legal duties.

## 4. RACI Controls

- Each activity must have one primary accountable role. Where `A*` applies, the applicable delegation must be confirmed in the decision record.
- Consultation must occur early enough to influence the decision, not merely after approval.
- An `R` role must produce or maintain evidence of completion.
- An `A` role must ensure the decision is recorded with owner, rationale, evidence, date, review date, and status.
- A role cannot be treated as accountable merely because it was informed.
- The RACI must be reviewed when the operating model, role structure, risk appetite, or AI portfolio changes.
- Role conflicts, vacancies, temporary delegations, and independence limitations must be recorded.
