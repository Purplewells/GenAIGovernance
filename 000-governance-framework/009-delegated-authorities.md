# AI Delegated Authorities Schedule

## 1. Purpose

This schedule defines the authority required for AI governance decisions. It is a proposed schedule and has no binding effect until approved by the Trust Board.

No person may accept risk, approve deployment, approve an exception, or authorise restart unless the decision falls within their recorded authority.

## 2. Authority Rules

- The highest applicable risk, impact, classification, and statutory duty determines the escalation route.
- A delegated authority cannot override law, professional duty, patient safety, or a prohibition.
- Where authority is unclear, the decision escalates to the AI Governance Committee and Trust Board.
- Where roles disagree, the higher risk classification and more restrictive decision apply until resolved.
- Delegation must be recorded by role and named post-holder, not inferred from attendance or consultation.
- A decision record must include owner, rationale, evidence, date, review date, status, conditions, and authority.

## 3. Proposed Authority Matrix

| Decision | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 or outside tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Register and triage | Executive Sponsor and Head of AI Governance | Same | Same | AI Governance Committee escalation |
| Initial classification | Head of AI Governance | Head of AI Governance | AI Governance Committee approval | Trust Board consideration |
| Approve design or procurement | System Owner and Executive Sponsor | AI Governance Committee or delegate | AI Governance Committee | Do not proceed unless Board authorises lawful exception |
| Approve controlled pilot | Executive Sponsor within approved conditions | AI Governance Committee | AI Governance Committee recommendation and authorised executive or Board approval | Do not proceed |
| Approve deployment | Executive Sponsor within approved conditions | AI Governance Committee or authorised executive | Trust Board or formally delegated executive following Committee recommendation | Do not proceed |
| Accept residual risk | Risk Owner within recorded tolerance | Authorised Risk Owner within delegated limit | Named authorised executive or Trust Board | Trust Board only, subject to law and specialist advice |
| Approve policy exception | Risk Owner within authority | AI Governance Committee | Trust Board or delegated executive | Trust Board; may be prohibited |
| Authorise restart after material incident | System Owner recommendation and Risk Owner | AI Governance Committee | AI Governance Committee and authorised executive or Board | Trust Board informed or approval required |
| Approve retirement | System Owner and Risk Owner | AI Governance Committee | AI Governance Committee recommendation | Trust Board where material or disputed |

These bands are proposed. The Trust Board must replace role descriptions with confirmed post-holders and delegated limits before operational use.

## 4. Reserved Matters

Trust Board approval is required for:

- Approval of this schedule, risk appetite, framework, policy, and approval process.
- Tier 3 or Tier 4 deployment with material patient, rights, public-interest, or reputational impact.
- Residual risk outside appetite or tolerance.
- Restart after a material incident where harm, rights, patient safety, or public trust was affected.
- Any exception that would permit activity otherwise prohibited by law or Trust policy.

## 5. Evidence and Review

Maintain the signed Board decision, delegation date, named post-holder, limits, conflicts, expiry, and review date. Review this schedule annually and after changes to the Board, committee terms, risk appetite, law, or AI portfolio.
